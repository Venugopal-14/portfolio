AutoOps AI — AIOps & Self-Healing Platform

Associated with my engineering work at BT Group. Confidential internal infrastructure details are intentionally omitted.

Core capabilities

  • Ingestion & deduplication: continuous alert ingestion with fingerprint-based deduplication to suppress noise.
  • Correlation & SLA tracking: alert-to-incident correlation, assignment and severity-based SLA tracking.
